📜 Ludo Rules

• Rolling a 6: Allows you to bring a token out of the base to the start tile AND gives you an extra roll!

• Capturing: Landing on an opponent's token sends it back to their base and grants you an immediate bonus roll.

• Safe Zones: Tiles marked with ★ Stars are safe zones where tokens cannot be captured.

• Victory: Navigate all 4 tokens around the board and into your home column to reach the central goal.

• Triple 6 Rule: Rolling three 6s in a row ends your turn without moving the 3rd roll.
